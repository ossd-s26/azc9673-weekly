# Week 15 Blog: Final Project Presentations

This week's classes were certainly an exciting time for all of us, as we finally got to exhibit all the hard work we've put into our projects for many weeks and see how the rest of our peers have done for theirs. Being that our group was the first to present out of everyone, I am proud to say we set the bar pretty high, not only in the content of our presentation (AKA the great contributions we made to Keycloak), but also in the way our presentation was clearly well practiced and smartly structured to include all of the important information we wanted to display within the given time. As for the other groups' presentations, it was cool to see how everyone clearly made meaningful contributions to all of their respective projects, and in general how the class itself was able to result in many valuable open source projects being improved thanks to the work of the students and the guidance of Professor Klukowska (with of course praise being deserved by the communities and maintainers of each of our projects as well).
